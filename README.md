GEO-BIAS IN LARGE LANGUAGE MODELS

MSc Data Science Dissertation
Middlesex University, UK

Project Overview

This dissertation looks at geographical bias in Large Language Models by comparing AI-generated responses with real-world air quality data and public perceptions.

The main aim was to understand whether the way different locations are represented by LLMs is similar to actual environmental data and the way people describe or perceive those locations.

The project involved:

• Real-world air quality data
• Public text and perception data
• Responses generated by Large Language Models
• Data cleaning and preparation
• Sentiment analysis
• Topic modelling
• Comparison and visualisation of the results

Large Language Models used:

• ChatGPT
• Claude
• Gemini
• Perplexity

The results from the different data sources were compared to identify patterns, differences and possible geographical bias in AI-generated responses.
